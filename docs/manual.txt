# Intro
Cat Crusher is a distortion, filter and compressor in one plugin. It makes drums, bass and leads thicker, grittier and louder: choose one of ten distortion types, add a hard digital crunch with Mech, shape the tone with a resonant 24 dB filter (low-pass, high-pass or band-pass), then glue everything with a one-knob compressor with automatic make-up gain, so the level stays the same at any Amount and only the density changes. A hidden soft clipper and safety limiter keep peaks under control. Built for dance, house, UK hardcore and hardstyle.

# Quick start
- Pick the preset closest to your sound (Fat Bass, Hardstyle Kick Crunch, UK Hardcore Stab, Gritty Drums).
- Set Type first, then raise Drive until it sounds right; the Distortion curve shows the shape you are using.
- Use Cutoff to remove harsh highs (Filter type Low-pass), thin the lows (High-pass) or make a telephone or radio sound (Band-pass), Amount for density, and Mix for parallel crush.
- Compare with the plugin bypassed and set Volume so both are equally loud.
- The round gear button in the header opens Settings: the plugin version, a link to the GitHub page and the releases page (it opens your browser; the plugin itself never goes online), and a switch for the info bubbles. Hover any control for about one second to see what it does; the round i button also turns the bubbles on or off.

# Recommendations
- Kick: Type Diode or Transistor, Drive 60 to 80, Phat mode on, Mix 80 to 90 to keep the click.
- Bass: Type Transistor, Drive 50, Cutoff 4 kHz, Phat mode on; keep Mech low so the sub stays clean.
- Radio or telephone: Filter type Band-pass, Cutoff 1 to 3 kHz, Resonance 30 to 60, Mech 40.
- Lead or stab: Mech 30 to 60 and Resonance 30 to 50 for screech; roll Cutoff down if it gets harsh.
- Drum bus: Type Tape, Drive 20 to 30, Amount 25 to 40, Mix 100 for glue or 60 to 70 for parallel punch.
- If it sounds louder but worse, lower Volume: loudness fools the ear.
